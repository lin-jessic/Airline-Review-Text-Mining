Advanced VADER Validation and Full Re-scoring Summary
========================================================================

Purpose
------------------------------------------------------------------------
This script validates and updates the VADER sentiment analysis method for the airline review project.
It compares original VADER and aviation-domain adjusted VADER, full-review input and sentence-average input,
and three thresholds: +/-0.05, +/-0.10, and +/-0.30.

Important Methodological Note
------------------------------------------------------------------------
This step only affects VADER sentiment analysis outputs. It does not modify TF-IDF, LDA, BERTopic, sampling, topic words, topic sizes, or representative reviews.
